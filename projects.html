Below are three major projects that demonstrate my experience with construction scheduling, concept development, and site safety planning.

PROJECT 01
Construction Scheduling

University Science Building Renovation

This project focused on developing a detailed construction schedule for a university science building renovation. I created task durations, identified dependencies, developed Gantt charts, and applied CPM analysis to support project planning, sequencing, and schedule communication.

PROJECT 02
Concept Development

Space Tourism Village for SpaceX

This project explored the planning and development of a space tourism village designed to support visitors near SpaceX operations. The work included project planning ideas, phased development concepts, operational needs, visitor access considerations, and the potential role of AI in improving management and tourism functions.

PROJECT 03
Construction Safety

Site Specific Safety Plan for University Science Building

This project involved creating a site specific safety plan for work related to the University Science Building. The plan addressed hazard identification, OSHA compliance, emergency procedures, and contractor safety coordination to help reduce risk during construction activities.
